FAQ
Questions people actually ask.
Still stuck? support@honestscanner.com — a person replies.
Is the free version actually usable?
Yes. Unlimited scans, unlimited pages, and a clean PDF export with no watermark. Pro adds searchable text and signatures — it doesn't unlock the basics.
Does OCR upload my documents?
No. Text recognition runs on device using Apple's Vision framework, so a contract you scan on a plane still comes out searchable.
One-time or subscription?
Your call. $29.99 once (Family Sharing included) or $19.99 a year. Both unlock exactly the same Pro features.
Where do my scans live?
In the app on your device. You can export to Files, Mail, or any share sheet target if you want a copy somewhere else — that choice stays with you.
Does it work on iPad?
Not in v1. iPad support with a proper split-view layout and Apple Pencil signing is on the roadmap for the next release.
