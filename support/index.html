Common questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?
Open Settings in the app → Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID that made the original purchase. Pro Lifetime supports Family Sharing.

I forgot my document password.
Password-protected PDFs cannot be recovered without the password. We never store, transmit, or receive your document passwords, so we cannot help decrypt a locked file. The original unlocked PDF was preserved in the app when you locked a copy — check the doc detail page for it.

Where are my scans stored?
On your iPhone only. Nothing is uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling the app deletes every scan.

Can I use this on iPad?
The first release is iPhone-only. Proper iPad support with a sidebar layout and Apple Pencil signing is planned for v1.1.

Do you have an Android version?
Not yet. iOS is our sole focus for v1.

A feature broke after an update.
Force-quit the app and reopen it. If the issue persists, email us with the app version (Settings → Version) and a short description.
