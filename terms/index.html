1. What Honest Scanner is

Honest Scanner turns paper into PDFs on your iPhone. Every step — capture, filter, OCR, PDF assembly, password protection, signature flatten, merge — happens on your device. There is no account, no cloud storage, no analytics, and no tracking.

2. Free vs. Pro

  • Free: unlimited scans, multi-page PDF export, filters, folders, local search over titles, share PDF, save to Files.
